# Bundled Citation Style Language (CSL) assets

`@kedataindo/docflow-plugins` compiles **Citation Style Language (CSL)** style
definitions and the **en-US CSL locale** into its `dist/` output. Those data are
**not** covered by DocFlow's Apache-2.0 license; they remain under their
upstream license, **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
(CC BY-SA 3.0)**.

## Attribution

- Citation Style Language project — <https://citationstyles.org>
- Styles: <https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles>
- Locales: <https://github.com/citation-style-language/locales>

Bundled styles include APA, Chicago author-date, Chicago notes & bibliography,
and MLA (Modern Language Association, 9th ed.), plus the en-US locale. The
individual style authors are listed in the `<author>` / `<contributor>` elements
of each upstream `.csl` file.
