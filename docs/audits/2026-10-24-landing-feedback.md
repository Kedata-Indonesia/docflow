## The 6 key points

1. **Move the best pitch up.** The sentence **"Real documents, without building Google Docs from scratch"** is currently buried under secondary text. Make it the leading subhead.

2. **Fix the primary CTA.** "Open Docflow Cloud" leads to a closed page because Cloud doesn't launch until Nov 4. Instead, direct high-intent developers to:
   - the `npm install` command,
   - the docs,
   - the GitHub repo.

3. **Highlight the interactive demo.** The footnote demo is a great feature, but users won't realize it's interactive without a prompt (e.g. **"click a note number"**) starting on page 1.

4. **Refine the comparison table.** The repeated "Paid add-on or not available" reads like hedging. Pull in brief explanations from the FAQ to make the table more transparent.

5. **Add social proof.** Include real metrics — star counts, npm downloads, or a link to the demo repo — to build credibility.

6. **Anchor pricing.** Give visitors a pricing idea (e.g. **"Early access from $X per seat"**) rather than only a launch date.

## What to keep (Scarlett's positive notes)

- Keep the **honest comparison framing**.
- Keep the line emphasizing **"no paid gate on pagination, footnotes, citations."**
